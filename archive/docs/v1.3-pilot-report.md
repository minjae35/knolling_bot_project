## Partial quality: valid geometry is not sufficient

All 30 Partial samples satisfy their instructions under both policies. Every
selected object physically moves or rotates in both versions; every locked object
has zero translation and rotation. Individual selected/locked IDs and movement
values are in `qc/partial_quality.csv`, `qc/object_movements.csv` and the HTML cards.
Movement counts use numerical tolerance; the existing supervised `move_label`
threshold (>3 mm or >3°) is also reported separately, without changing its meaning.

| Selected objects / physically moved objects | Samples |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 11 |
| 2 | 11 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 6 |

The **11 single-object cases (36.7%)** teach visual selection, preservation of
others and repositioning/reorientation. They cannot supervise relationships
between selected objects, so they should not count as multi-object knolling
evidence. The other **19 cases (63.3%)** supervise local arrangements of 2–4
objects. That is meaningful pilot coverage for this narrower task, but does not
establish generalization or robust interactive refinement.

There are **zero structurally organized whole-source scenes**, **zero documented
organized-source refinement episodes**, and **zero multi-turn episodes** among
these 30. Sources come from the original randomized-scatter generator. Locked
objects must remain scattered if they began that way; moving them to make the
whole scene look neat would violate the instruction.

The mean per-scene mean squared selected-center displacement is reduced from
**0.03963919 to 0.01629424 m² (58.89%)**, with no individual Partial increase.
This measures movement cost, not a 58.89% aesthetic or neural improvement.
Minimum movement is over the finite row-template/5 mm lattice candidate family,
not unrestricted continuous optimization or robot trajectory length.

Partial J_shape improves in 3 cases, is unchanged in 20, and worsens in 7:
`scene_0027_primary`, `scene_0033_primary`, `scene_0034_primary`,
`scene_0064_primary`, `scene_0068_primary`, `scene_0081_primary`,
`scene_0088_primary`. This is compatible with movement-first selection but must
remain visible in manual review. Full J_shape improves in 27/80 cases and is
unchanged in 53; lower J_shape is not proof of better aesthetics. For example,
small object sets may change from one horizontal row to a vertical stack of rows
while still obeying horizontal object-axis alignment.

**Minimal recommendation, not applied:** retain the current 130 samples and their
distribution. Keep the 11 single-object examples as selection/preservation
supervision. If interactive refinement is the next coverage goal, separately add
8–12 single-step examples with organized sources, 2–4 selected objects and locked
context, including gap/axis/grouping edits. Preserve lineage splits and review
each addition. This does not require multi-turn data before the initial neural
overfit pilot. No such additional data were generated here.
